Fluxr Agents Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fluxr, based in Johannesburg, South Africa, collects and uses personal information when you use the Fluxr Agents mobile application, agent web portal, and related services. In this policy, these products are referred to collectively as the "Service".

1. About This Policy

This policy applies to people who register as Fluxr agents and to the limited information we hold about customers referred by those agents. It should be read together with any terms that apply to the Service.

The Service is intended for people who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that a child has provided personal information, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the personal information needed to register you, verify your identity, administer the agent programme, and pay commission. The information we collect may include:

  • your name, title, mobile number, optional email address, date of birth, gender, nationality, and residential address;
  • your South African identity number, or your passport number and country of issue;
  • photographs of your identity document and a selfie used to verify your identity;
  • your bank account details or mobile number for receiving approved payouts;
  • records of referrals, commission, wallet activity, withdrawals, and payout references;
  • account and security information, including your protected app PIN and sign-in verification records;
  • basic device and service information, such as your device type, IP address, access times, and information needed to deliver notifications; and
  • information you choose to provide, including feedback and nicknames for referred customers.

We do not ask for card numbers, banking passwords, or internet banking credentials. The app does not collect your precise location, address book, call logs, messages, or browsing history. We do not use advertising trackers in the Service.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • create, maintain, and secure your agent account;
  • verify your identity and meet our obligations under the Financial Intelligence Centre Act, 2001 (FICA);
  • record referrals, calculate commission, and process withdrawals;
  • communicate with you about verification, payments, security, and important Service updates;
  • prevent fraud, misuse, and unauthorised access;
  • respond to support requests and feedback; and
  • maintain and improve the reliability of the Service.

We process personal information where it is necessary to provide the Service or perform our agreement with you, where the law requires it, where we have a legitimate business interest, or where you have given consent. We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

4. Who We Share Information With

We share personal information only where it is necessary to provide the Service, complete a payment, protect our users, or comply with the law. Recipients may include:

  • hosting and storage providers that keep the Service available and secure;
  • Google Firebase, if you choose to receive app notifications;
  • SMS providers that deliver verification and service messages;
  • banking, wallet, voucher, and other payout partners that complete withdrawals; and
  • regulators, courts, law enforcement agencies, or other authorities when disclosure is required by law.

These providers may use personal information only to provide services to Fluxr or as the law permits. If Fluxr is restructured, sold, or transferred, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to appropriate confidentiality and data protection safeguards.

5. International Processing

Some of our service providers process information outside South Africa. For example, our cloud infrastructure may store information in the United Kingdom. Where personal information is transferred across borders, we take reasonable steps to ensure that it receives a level of protection consistent with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

6. How We Protect Personal Information

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, and disclosure. These safeguards include secure connections, restricted access to sensitive records, private storage for identity documents, and controls designed to protect agent accounts.

Your app PIN is stored in a protected form and cannot be read by Fluxr staff. No system is completely secure, but we regularly review our safeguards. If a security incident affects your personal information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator where required by law.

7. Information About Referred Customers

When you refer a customer, we record the connection between your agent account and the customer's mobile number so that we can attribute the referral and calculate commission. The app masks part of the customer's number. A nickname you add is visible only within your agent account and should not contain sensitive or unnecessary information.

Other personal information about referred customers is handled under the privacy terms that apply to the Fluxr customer service they use.

8. How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy, or for as long as the law requires. In particular:

  • identity verification records are kept for at least five years after the agent relationship ends, as required by FICA;
  • commission, wallet, withdrawal, and payment records are kept for the periods required by financial, tax, and other applicable laws; and
  • security and service records are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to protect and operate the Service.

When information is no longer required, we delete it or remove identifying details so that it can no longer be linked to an individual.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to POPIA and other applicable laws, you may:

  • ask whether we hold personal information about you and request access to it;
  • ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date;
  • ask us to delete information that we are not legally required to keep;
  • object to certain uses of your personal information;
  • withdraw consent where our processing relies on your consent; and
  • complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforeg@justice.gov.za.

To exercise a privacy right, email info@fluxr.co.za. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

10. Account Deletion and Your Choices

You may request permanent deletion of your agent account and associated personal data. See Delete your Fluxr Agents account for the request steps, processing time, and information we must retain. You can email info@fluxr.co.za if you cannot access the app. We will disable access and delete personal information that we no longer need. Information that must be retained for legal, regulatory, fraud prevention, or financial record-keeping purposes will be kept only for the required period.

You can also control optional app permissions:

  • Camera and photo access: used when you choose to capture or select identity verification images.
  • Notifications: optional and used for Service updates. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services, legal obligations, or privacy practices change. The latest version will remain available on this page and will show its effective date. If a change materially affects how we use personal information, we will provide appropriate notice through the app, by SMS, or through another suitable channel.
