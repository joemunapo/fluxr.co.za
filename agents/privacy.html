Fluxr Agents Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fluxr ("Fluxr", "we", "us"), of Johannesburg, South Africa, collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use the Fluxr Agents mobile application, the Fluxr agent web portal, and related agent services (together, the "Service"). It is written to comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA), the Financial Intelligence Centre Act, 2001 (FICA), and Google Play's User Data policies.

Your agreement

By registering as a Fluxr agent, you confirm that you have read and understood this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Service.

1. Who This Policy Covers

  • Agents — people who register on the Fluxr Agents app to refer customers and earn commission.
  • Referred customers — Fluxr customers whom an agent introduces. We hold limited information about them in connection with the agent programme (see section 6).

The Service is intended for persons 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18; if we learn we have done so, we will delete it.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what the Service needs to operate. We do not collect your location, contacts, call logs, messages, or browsing history, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics trackers.

2.1 Registration and profile information

  • Full name and title
  • Mobile phone number (your account identifier, verified by SMS one-time PIN)
  • Email address (optional)
  • Date of birth, gender, nationality, and residential address

2.2 Identity verification (FICA/KYC) information

As a financial-services agent programme, we are legally required to verify your identity before paying you. For this we collect:

  • South African ID number, or passport number and country of issue
  • Photographs of your identity document (ID front and back, or passport photo page) and a selfie photograph

These images are uploaded over an encrypted connection, stored in private cloud storage that is never publicly accessible, and are viewable only by authorised Fluxr staff for verification.

2.3 Payout and financial information

  • Bank name, account number and account type, and/or the mobile number for wallet-based payouts
  • Your commission, wallet, and withdrawal history, including payout references and, for voucher payouts, the voucher reference and PIN issued to you
  • We do not collect card numbers, and we never ask for your banking passwords or internet-banking credentials.

2.4 Security credentials

  • A 6-digit app PIN, stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash — we cannot read your PIN
  • SMS one-time PINs used during sign-in (short-lived and deleted after use)

2.5 Device and technical information

  • A device identifier, device platform, and — if you allow notifications — a Firebase Cloud Messaging push token
  • Standard technical logs (such as IP address, timestamps, and request details) generated when the app communicates with our servers, used for security, rate-limiting, and troubleshooting

2.6 Information you submit in the app

  • Feedback or ideas you send through the in-app Suggestion Box
  • Optional display names ("nicknames") you assign to your referred customers

4. Who We Share Information With

We share personal information only with service providers who process it on our instructions, and only as far as needed to run the Service:

  • Cloud hosting and storage — our servers and private file storage run on reputable cloud infrastructure (Amazon Web Services, hosted in the AWS eu-west-2 (London) region via our hosting platform).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — if you enable notifications, your push token is processed by Google to deliver data-only push messages. These messages carry no personal information — they only tell the app to refresh.
  • SMS delivery providers — to send one-time PINs and service SMS to your phone number.
  • Payout partners — when you withdraw, the details needed to complete that payout (for example, the amount and, for wallet transfers, the recipient mobile number) are shared with the relevant payout or voucher partner.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it — for example, to the Financial Intelligence Centre, a regulator, or a court — or to protect the rights, safety, and property of Fluxr, our agents, or our customers.

Where information is processed outside South Africa (such as cloud hosting in the United Kingdom), we ensure the recipient is subject to laws or agreements providing a substantially similar level of protection, as required by POPIA.

5. How We Protect Your Information

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Identity documents and selfies are stored in private cloud storage; they are never given public links and are viewable only by authorised reviewers.
  • Your app PIN is stored as a one-way hash; voucher PINs are only shown to you inside your authenticated account and sent to your verified number.
  • Access to production systems is restricted, logged, and role-based.
  • Repeated failed sign-in and PIN attempts are limited and locked out.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach occurs that affects your personal information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as required by POPIA.

6. Referred Customers

When you refer a customer, we record the link between your agent account and that customer's mobile number in order to calculate your commission. In the app, referred customers are shown to you with their number partially masked; any nickname you assign to a customer is visible only to you and is used for no other purpose. Referred customers' personal information is otherwise governed by the privacy terms of the Fluxr customer service they use. Commission records reference the transaction, not the contents of the customer's activity.

You must not enter another person's information (for example, a nickname that includes sensitive detail) beyond what is needed to recognise the customer.

7. How Long We Keep Information

  • Identity verification records — kept for at least five (5) years after your agent relationship ends, as required by FICA.
  • Financial records (commissions, wallet ledger, withdrawals) — kept for the periods required by South African tax and financial-records law.
  • Technical logs — kept for short rolling periods needed for security and troubleshooting.
  • Push tokens — deleted when you sign out, uninstall the app, or when the token becomes invalid.
  • SMS one-time PINs — deleted shortly after they expire or are used.

When a retention period ends, records are deleted or irreversibly de-identified.

8. Your Rights

Under POPIA you may, at any time:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information (identity and payout details can also be corrected in the app before verification, and by contacting us after);
  • ask us to delete information we are not legally required to keep;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent for push notifications (disable them in your device settings at any time);
  • complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforeg@justice.gov.za if you believe we have processed your information unlawfully.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@fluxr.co.za. We will respond within a reasonable time and verify your identity before acting.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your agent account at any time by contacting info@fluxr.co.za from your registered details or through the in-app Suggestion Box. On a deletion request we will:

  1. close your agent account and disable sign-in;
  2. delete personal information we are not legally obliged to keep; and
  3. retain only what FICA and financial-records law require us to keep, for the periods in section 7, after which it is deleted.

10. App Permissions

The app requests only the permissions it needs:

  • Camera / photo access — used solely to capture or select your identity document photographs and selfie for verification, and only when you initiate an upload.
  • Notifications — optional; used for data-only push messages that keep the app's information current.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at this page with its effective date. If a change materially affects how we handle your personal information, we will notify you in the app or by SMS before it takes effect.
