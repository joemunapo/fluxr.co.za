Fluxr App Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fluxr collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Fluxr mobile app, Android package za.co.fluxr.app, and the API-backed wallet, funding, support, and service transaction features connected to it.

1. Who We Are

Fluxr is operated from Johannesburg, South Africa. For privacy questions, account deletion requests, or data requests, contact us at info@fluxr.co.za or WhatsApp +27 60 636 0061.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information needed to create your account, secure your wallet, provide services, process transactions, and support you.

  • Account information such as your name, phone number, optional email address, verification status, OTP activity, login PIN status, and authentication tokens.
  • Wallet and transaction information such as balances, transaction references, amounts, statuses, receipts, recipient numbers, notes, voucher funding details, bank eWallet redemption details, crypto deposit references, and service-specific details.
  • Service details you submit, including airtime or bundle recipient numbers, meter numbers, smart-card numbers, bill or account references, order references, customer names returned by providers, voucher email addresses, voucher codes, serial numbers, and redemption instructions.
  • Support information such as issue type, support message, ticket reference, phone and email snapshots, and support channel activity.
  • Security and technical information such as IP address, user agent, failed PIN or OTP attempts, daily-limit checks, private security flags, and a hashed device ID when supplied by the app.
  • Device feature data when you choose to use it, such as a selected phone number from your contacts, a voucher scan image sent for PIN extraction, locally stored biometric login settings, local session data, and receipt-sharing output created on your device.

3. How We Use Information

  • To register and authenticate users, send OTP or PIN recovery messages, maintain sessions, and protect accounts from unauthorized access.
  • To show wallet balance, history, receipts, transaction status, and service availability.
  • To process wallet deposits, wallet transfers, airtime, bundles, electricity, DStv, voucher purchases, topups, send-money payments, and bill payments.
  • To run security checks, enforce outgoing limits, prevent fraud, investigate failed or pending transactions, and comply with legal, tax, audit, and operational requirements.
  • To provide customer support, troubleshoot service issues, improve reliability, and communicate important service or policy updates.

4. App Permissions and Device Features

The app uses internet access to communicate with Fluxr services. If your device supports biometrics, you may enable biometric login or biometric transaction confirmation. Biometric matching is handled by your device; Fluxr does not receive your fingerprint, face scan, or biometric template.

Some fields may offer a contact picker so you can choose a recipient number. The app uses the selected number for that form and does not upload your full address book. Voucher scan images are used to extract voucher PIN digits; they are not redeemed by scanning and are not stored by default after processing.

5. Sharing and Service Providers

We share only the information needed to provide, secure, verify, or support the service you request.

  • Telecom, network, voucher, redemption, bill-payment, wallet, and service providers that complete purchases, deposits, lookups, or transfers.
  • SMS and OTP providers that deliver verification, login, recovery, or transaction messages.
  • CryptoPay and related crypto infrastructure providers when you use USDT BEP20 crypto deposit features.
  • OCR, storage, hosting, monitoring, support, and administration systems that help operate the app and API securely.
  • Authorities, regulators, courts, or other parties when required by law, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or a business transfer with appropriate notice.

Fluxr does not sell personal and sensitive user data.

6. Security

We use secure transmission, protected authentication flows, hashed or restricted security identifiers where appropriate, operational access controls, and admin-only access for sensitive support and transaction information. You are responsible for keeping your phone, PIN, device lock, and account access secure.

7. Retention and Deletion

We keep account, wallet, transaction, provider, support, fraud-prevention, tax, audit, and compliance records for as long as needed to operate Fluxr, resolve disputes, meet legal requirements, and protect users. When information is no longer needed, we delete, anonymize, or securely archive it according to our operational and legal obligations.

You can request account deletion in the mobile app from Profile > Delete Account, through our app account deletion page, or by contacting info@fluxr.co.za. Deletion requests may disable access while we process the request. Some records may be retained where required for fraud prevention, financial reconciliation, legal compliance, security, dispute handling, or audit purposes.

8. Children, Ads, and Marketing

The Fluxr mobile app is not directed to children. We do not use the app to sell personal data, and we do not use personal and sensitive user data for advertising sale or advertising personalization. If this changes, we will update this policy and any required app disclosures before collecting or using data in that way.

9. Cross-Border Processing

Fluxr may process and store information in South Africa or in other countries where our infrastructure, support systems, or service providers operate. We take steps designed to protect personal information according to this policy wherever it is processed.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, API, providers, legal requirements, or data practices change. The updated version will show a new last updated date. Continued use of the app after an update means the updated policy applies to your use of Fluxr.
